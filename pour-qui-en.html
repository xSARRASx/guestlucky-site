Licensed property manager

You manage other people's properties. With pro-grade standards.

You hold a real-estate license, a written agreement with every owner, cleaning teams, service providers and separate bookkeeping for each owner-client. You want a tool that speaks your industry's language, not a glorified spreadsheet.

  • Dedicated owner portal - each owner sees their bookings, revenue and statements live. No more 10 WhatsApp messages a week.
