We've gathered everything people ask us.

Pricing, how the Channel Manager works, guest AI, migration, compliance, security… if an answer is missing, message us on WhatsApp. We reply in under 5 min.

PricingHow much does it really cost?

3 clear plans. No surprises.

• Premium - €5.99 excl. tax/month/property. Everything except the Channel Manager and the AI.
• Pro - €14.99 excl. tax/month/property (or €149/year). Everything: CM + Booking Engine + Lucky Copilot + guest AI + WhatsApp cleaning.
• Custom - on quote for 50+ properties.

Want to see the tool first? Book a free demo. See full pricing details →

PricingIs it true you take no commission?

Yes. 0% commission on everything: deposits, additional services (late check-out, extra cleaning), guest upsells, direct payments. The money goes 100% to your Stripe account.

You only pay your monthly subscription. No hidden percentages, no disguised "transaction fee". Ever.

PricingWhat if I go over 2 properties?

You automatically switch to Premium or Pro depending on your choice. You only pay for active properties (an archived property is free). The transition is invisible: no data loss, no reconfiguration.

PricingHow long do I have to commit?

No commitment. All plans are monthly and cancelable in 2 clicks from your account. The annual plan (-20%) is available if you want to save.

ProductWhat's included in the Pro plan?

Everything. Pro includes the Channel Manager (Airbnb, Booking, Abritel, Vrbo), 24/7 guest AI messaging in 6 languages, Lucky Copilot (the manager AI), cleaning operated via WhatsApp, the dedicated owner interface, 2026 e-invoicing, and priority WhatsApp support.

ProductHow does the Channel Manager work?

You connect your Airbnb, Booking, Abritel accounts to GuestLucky in 5 minutes (OAuth or API). A booking on one channel blocks the others in under 2 seconds: zero double-booking guaranteed. Prices, calendars, availabilities synced in real time.

We talk directly to the channels - no third-party API that could crash.

ProductDoes the guest AI really work?

Yes, and you have 3 modes depending on your comfort level:

• Manual validation - the AI suggests, you approve in 1 click
• Auto with filters - it sends unless it detects a sensitive topic (refund, dispute, emergency)
• 100% auto - for experienced hosts, it handles everything

It speaks 6 languages, in your tone of voice (you show it 5 examples, it adapts). You can interrupt any conversation from the app.

ProductWhat exactly is Lucky Copilot?

An AI assistant for you, not for guests. It acts in your account: creates a listing, lowers a price, generates an incident, pulls an accounting export, schedules a cleaning.

Concrete examples:

• "Lower the price of Loft République by 10% between July 14 and 21"
• "Give me June's Airbnb revenue with VAT deducted"
• "Create a clogged shower incident at Sarah K.'s, with her photo"

Available in the app and on WhatsApp. Included from the Pro plan.

ProductHow many channels does GuestLucky support?

The 4 main ones on the French market: Airbnb, Booking.com, Abritel/Vrbo, and Direct (direct bookings via your own site or by phone). We evaluate other channels based on demand.

ProductDo you offer a direct booking website?

Yes, via our partner Booking In Shine: a website customized to your brand, with no commission, native sync with your Channel Manager. Optional add-on, we'll detail the price during the demo.

ProductAnd how does cleaning over WhatsApp work?

Your cleaning providers receive their assignments directly on WhatsApp - no app to install, no training. Mission auto-assigned at check-out, built-in checklist, before/after photos pushed back into the booking record.

It works from day one: they already know how to use WhatsApp, you have nothing to explain.

MigrationHow does migration from my current tool work?

Free, usually done in 48 hours. Our team takes over your listings, calendars, guest templates, provider contacts. We support every tool on the market - we've already migrated hundreds of accounts.

You have nothing to do except give us read access. We send you a Loom as soon as everything is ready.

MigrationIs it suitable for beginners?

Yes. The interface is in plain English, built for non-technical people. And we offer a free setup session starting with the Premium plan: we configure your first property over video call, and you leave with a working account.

MigrationHow many properties does it work for?

From 1 to 500+. Premium or Pro with no limit (you pay per property). Over 50 properties, you switch to the Custom plan with a dedicated account manager and a negotiated rate.

MigrationCan I discover the tool before paying?

Yes, 2 ways, with no credit card:

You see concretely how GuestLucky fits your business, no pressure and no commitment. When you pick your plan (Premium or Pro), our team handles the Channel Manager connections for you.

No service interruption. You keep working while we take over in the background.

MigrationWhat's included in the paid onboarding?

If you choose the personalized onboarding option (€47), you leave with a fully configured account, not just with the connections done:

  • Listings: detailed configuration of each property (descriptions, photos, amenities, house rules)
  • Additional services: deposit, cleaning, late check-out, baby kits, etc.
  • Team members: creation of provider accounts (cleaning, concierge, owners) with the right permissions
  • Stock & inventory: linen, consumables, restocking alerts
  • Welcome book: interactive template in 6 languages, ready to send
  • Auto-actions: the 10 standard ones (Wi-Fi code at D-1, review request at D+1, etc.)

The onboarding is done over video call with a GuestLucky expert. The video is recorded and sent back to you so you can replay it anytime. After that, you also have access to the Skool community and the daily free workshops to go further.

SecurityIs my data secure?

Hosting in France (PlanetHoster Roubaix datacenter), TLS 1.3 encryption, payments via Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1), daily backups with 30-day retention, native GDPR compliance.

You can export or delete 100% of your data in 1 click.

SecurityDo you handle 2026 e-invoicing?

Yes - natively. Certified PDF invoice, auto-sent to the guest, 10-year archiving, deposited on Chorus Pro. We've been tracking the reform from day one, we're ready ahead of the official deadline.

SecurityAnd Hoguet law (Carte G) compliance?

The tool is built for professional property management firms holding the Carte G: operations register, separate accounting per principal, 10-year archiving, digitized contracts. We speak your trade's language, not the other way around.

SecurityWhat happens to my data if I stop?

You can export 100% at any time (bookings, guests, listings, accounting). If you delete your account, your data is erased within 30 days. No trace, no "vendor lock-in", you leave whenever you want.

SupportIs support handled by bots or humans?

Humans. A small French team, based in France. Reachable on WhatsApp (our preferred channel, reply in 2-5 min during the day), by email (within 24 business hours), or via the ? button in the app.

No chatbot, no offshore BPO. Your ticket lands straight in the team's Slack.

SupportWhat are the support hours?

Mon-Fri, 9 a.m.-7 p.m. Paris time. On weekends, we still keep an eye on active guest emergencies (blocked check-in, broken payment). For the Custom plan, you get a direct contact reachable outside business hours.
