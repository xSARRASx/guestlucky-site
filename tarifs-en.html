Your questions, our answers.

Is there any commitment?

None. You can cancel anytime from your account, no justification, no fees.

What's the difference between Premium and Pro?

Premium includes everything except the Channel Manager and the AI features. Pro adds the Channel Manager (Airbnb, Booking, Abritel), the guest AI messaging, Lucky Copilot and the Smart Locks integration (Igloohome). Lucky Clean (WhatsApp cleaning ops) is an optional paid add-on, quoted on demand.

What does "0% commission" mean?

All the deposits, add-on services and guest payments you collect through GuestLucky go 100% to your Stripe account. We take no commission, on anything. Ever.

How much does it cost to migrate from my current tool?

Nothing. Our team handles the migration for you, for free, usually in 48h. We bring over your listings, calendars, guest templates - no matter where you're coming from.

What if I have more than 50 properties?

We switch to negotiated pricing + a dedicated account manager. Get in touch so we can evaluate together: via the contact page or WhatsApp 07 59 94 43 05.
